Which Historical Archetype Are You?

History is full of recurring human roles: the builder, the explorer, the reformer, the thinker, and more. Answer 15 questions to discover the two archetypes that best match how you approach the world.

How the quiz works

Every answer secretly awards points to two different archetypes. Your strongest match receives 2 points and a related archetype receives 1 point.

Your highest score becomes your Primary Archetype. Your second-highest score becomes your Supporting Archetype. Together, they form a more complete historical personality profile.

This is not a scientific personality test. It is a fun way to think about your strengths while meeting recurring types of people who will appear throughout World History.

Before you begin

Choose the answer that sounds most like you—not the answer you think sounds most impressive.
